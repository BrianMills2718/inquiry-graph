# Changelog

## Unreleased

Expanded the founding dialogue through the epistemic-transition, assumption-context, metareasoning/reflection, candidate-generation, and warrant/license refinements. The curated source remains at 219 excerpts and the proposed graph at 229 content nodes, 250 relations, 185 moves, 58 stance events, and 76 question events. A subsequent literature-driven research pass adds an epistemic-action/support algebra without extending the source-grounded dialogue graph: epistemic actions are typed partial state transducers with representation-relative generator bases; positive support provenance preserves alternative versus joint support symbolically; graded support is an indexed interpretation of that provenance rather than a universal scalar; and heterogeneous licenses remain typed unless an explicit meta-regime combines them. The positive-support choice is concrete and executable: finite antichains of minimal assumption environments form the free-distributive-lattice support algebra, with a reference independent-Bernoulli interpretation conditioned on ATMS-style nogoods. Defeasible conflict is now layered rather than encoded as signed support: supported arguments participate in typed rebut/undercut/undermine defeats, and an experimental Dung grounded-semantics module computes IN/OUT/UNDECIDED acceptability while preserving the underlying positive support. A six-case ABA/ASPIC+ benchmark selects an ABA-first executable bridge while preserving ASPIC+-compatible attack-origin and rule-applicability metadata. A minimal ABA core is now implemented with Horn-style rules, assumptions/contraries, minimal-support argument construction, typed attacks, reversible defeasible-rule translation, and projection to the Dung defeat layer. The argument-identity boundary is explicitly resolved: the ABA dialectical layer keeps the conclusion + assumption-support quotient, while exact derivation/proof identity remains a separate richer representation and an explicit escalation path for ASPIC+-level or proof-sensitive semantics. Preference-sensitive binary defeat now uses a Dung-compatible normal-attack filter with transitive strict assumption preferences and auditable blocked attacks. Full ABA+ reversal remains an explicit set-to-set/hyperargumentation escalation path rather than being approximated by binary edge reversal. The first executable warrant/license specialization now requires grounded-IN dialectical certificates, typed defeasible action/guarantee scope, and explicit context-assumption satisfaction before deriving a license. An end-to-end regression benchmark covers unchallenged support, defeat, preference blocking, applicability failure, alternative support routes, and preservation of positive support across preference changes. A second executable warrant regime now computes and licenses recording an independent-Bernoulli support-event probability while explicitly refusing to turn the numeric grade into proposition acceptance. The warrant certificate interface is generalized beyond argument IDs. A checked strict-Horn proof fragment and deductive warrant regime now license derivation relative to explicit premises while preserving the distinction between proof validity and premise acceptance. Narrow measurement and testimony warrant regimes now preserve measurement uncertainty/calibration provenance and reference-class-relative source reliability while refusing to turn either evidence channel directly into proposition acceptance. A finite-class uniform-convergence warrant regime now records a checked Hoeffding/union-bound generalization bound under explicit sampling assumptions without promoting the theorem directly to predictor-use permission. A paired bounded-utility strategy-performance warrant now licenses strategy selection only when a positive lower confidence bound over a declared task class is established. The project has reached an architecture checkpoint: further formal-layer expansion is benchmark-driven rather than automatic. Integrated native-Windows verification established a green 138-test baseline; PR #36 subsequently adds multiple-comparison and optional-stopping handling to the strategy-performance reference regime and reports 142 passing tests on its tested branch. Candidate generation, cross-framework orchestration, and formal guarantee transport are now substantially mapped to mature prior work. Current open work centers on acceptance licensing/warrant composition (#34), systematic comparison/adoption against the closest integrated frameworks (#40), source reconciliation (#3), empirical usefulness of the inquiry representation/system (#38), and dormant escalation paths (#17/#18) only if concrete cases trigger them.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-27

Initial local-first inquiry graph toolkit: typed source-grounded schema, reified relations/moves, actor stance and question histories, branch-aware export import, structured-response ingestion, optional provider adapter, structural validator, exact-identity union, queries and offline views. Includes the founding conversation's curated first pass, requirements/formalism/ontology/architecture/review/evaluation/security documentation, ADRs and tests.

Limitations: no full-export reconciliation, no independently reviewed gold graph, no live paid extraction, no automatic semantic identity merge, no trained reasoning policy, no deployed web service or graph database.
