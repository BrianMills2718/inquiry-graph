# Changelog

## Unreleased

Expanded the founding dialogue through the epistemic-transition, assumption-context, metareasoning/reflection, candidate-generation, and warrant/license refinements. The curated source remains at 219 excerpts and the proposed graph at 229 content nodes, 250 relations, 185 moves, 58 stance events, and 76 question events. A subsequent literature-driven research pass adds an epistemic-action/support algebra without extending the source-grounded dialogue graph: epistemic actions are typed partial state transducers with representation-relative generator bases; positive support provenance preserves alternative versus joint support symbolically; graded support is an indexed interpretation of that provenance rather than a universal scalar; and heterogeneous licenses remain typed unless an explicit meta-regime combines them. The positive-support choice is concrete and executable: finite antichains of minimal assumption environments form the free-distributive-lattice support algebra, with a reference independent-Bernoulli interpretation conditioned on ATMS-style nogoods. Defeasible conflict is now layered rather than encoded as signed support: supported arguments participate in typed rebut/undercut/undermine defeats, and an experimental Dung grounded-semantics module computes IN/OUT/UNDECIDED acceptability while preserving the underlying positive support. A six-case ABA/ASPIC+ benchmark selects an ABA-first executable bridge while preserving ASPIC+-compatible attack-origin and rule-applicability metadata. A minimal ABA core is now implemented with Horn-style rules, assumptions/contraries, minimal-support argument construction, typed attacks, reversible defeasible-rule translation, and projection to the Dung defeat layer. The argument-identity boundary is explicitly resolved: the ABA dialectical layer keeps the conclusion + assumption-support quotient, while exact derivation/proof identity remains a separate richer representation and an explicit escalation path for ASPIC+-level or proof-sensitive semantics. Preference-sensitive binary defeat now uses a Dung-compatible normal-attack filter with transitive strict assumption preferences and auditable blocked attacks. Full ABA+ reversal remains an explicit set-to-set/hyperargumentation escalation path rather than being approximated by binary edge reversal. The first executable warrant/license specialization now requires grounded-IN dialectical certificates, typed defeasible action/guarantee scope, and explicit context-assumption satisfaction before deriving a license. An end-to-end regression benchmark covers unchallenged support, defeat, preference blocking, applicability failure, alternative support routes, and preservation of positive support across preference changes. Open work now centers on expanding guarantee-regime coverage (deductive, graded, measurement/testimony, statistical/PAC, strategy), action-algebra representation theorems, and robust strategy-episode identification.

## 1.0.0 — 2026-09-27

Initial local-first inquiry graph toolkit: typed source-grounded schema, reified relations/moves, actor stance and question histories, branch-aware export import, structured-response ingestion, optional provider adapter, structural validator, exact-identity union, queries and offline views. Includes the founding conversation's curated first pass, requirements/formalism/ontology/architecture/review/evaluation/security documentation, ADRs and tests.

Limitations: no full-export reconciliation, no independently reviewed gold graph, no live paid extraction, no automatic semantic identity merge, no trained reasoning policy, no deployed web service or graph database.
