## Inquiry trajectory

1. **Cosmic lore and textual evidence.** The initial questions concerned whether Gurdjieff taught parasitic spiritual forces, how the Moon and Sun receive nourishment, whether the extraterrestrial mythology was literal, and how lecture records compare with literary narration.
2. **Visual explanation.** The inquiry moved from understanding the lore to presenting cosmological mechanisms as animated explanations, while limiting repetitive on-screen epistemic disclaimers.
3. **Ontology proposal.** A top-level ontology was proposed as a way to organize consistent visual meanings; its suitability was questioned.
4. **Conceptual interlingua.** The user reframed the architecture: translate each tradition's terms into an intermediate conceptual representation, then map that to visuals. Terminological resemblance should neither establish nor obscure conceptual equivalence.
5. **Compositional semantics.** Geometry, connectivity, motion, texture, and other visual dimensions should carry reusable meanings where possible; iconic depiction and conventional symbols remain allowed.
6. **Consciousness and agency.** The representation must separately express subjective experience, awareness, deliberation, decisions, moral agency, and metaphysical freedom, without imposing one philosophy of mind.
7. **Software and reuse.** A v0.2 conceptual corpus was approved; `lore-interlingua` was created, and `visual-language` was examined as a related but separate notation research project.
8. **Methodological revision.** Instead of trying to finalize the ontology upfront or to generate complete movies solely from grammar, work should iterate through concrete scenes, discover failures, reuse conventions, and allow artistic direction.

## Active working decisions

- Keep **conceptual meaning**, **visual grammar**, and **art direction** as distinct layers.
- Optimize for semantic consistency, reuse, and comprehension—not total automatic generation.
- Employ critical project management; challenge unsound assumptions rather than agreeing automatically.
- Maintain a single opening attribution/disclaimer in finished lore videos; keep source scrutiny in the research workflow.
- Allow iconic representations and learned symbols where pure composition is insufficient.
- Use existing `visual-language` research as potential prior art, not as an automatic replacement for the interlingua.
- Keep the interlingua and notation extensible through specific observed representational failures.

These are descriptions of positions and decisions expressed in the discussion, **not assertions about the truth of Gurdjieff's cosmology**.
