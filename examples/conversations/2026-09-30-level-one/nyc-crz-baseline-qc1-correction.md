# NYC baseline correction — NYC-QC-1 was recoverable

## Correction

The WP0/WP1 baseline recorded the six-hearing qualitative result as missing because the then-read Workbench planning state said the QC lane had stalled.

A direct machine/repository audit later found:

- the QC implementation had been merged to `qualitative_coding/main`;
- an authentic completed six-hearing project artifact was retained outside the default QC project store;
- the project completed the full relevant pipeline;
- an independent claim-discipline review was also retained.

Therefore the earlier statement:

> “the six-hearing qualitative method result is missing”

is superseded by:

> **the method-owned result exists; what is still missing/unverified is separate human acceptance and Workbench integration of that result.**

## Why the historical baseline is not edited

This is itself a useful revision test.

The new project-state evidence should:
- reopen the “missing QC strand” node;
- add the recovered method artifact and its review;
- leave the agency prediction, quantitative arithmetic, medical-access source claim, and air-quality update untouched.

That is exactly the selective-revision behavior the prior NYC exercise was designed to test.

## Methodological consequence

The recovery also exposes a stronger problem than absence: the raw synthesis overclaimed. Independent review marked 22 of 34 reviewed outputs as violating declared limits.

So “result exists” does not mean “raw synthesis is acceptable.”

The integration must consume the **bounded/reviewed result**, preserving:
- provenance;
- review verdict;
- rejected formulations;
- claim limits;
- method ownership.

This gives the federation a real method-produced artifact rather than a hand-constructed placeholder.
