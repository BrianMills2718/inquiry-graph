## Coverage and status

This is **not** a complete native ChatGPT export. It preserves selected visible excerpts that establish the main questions, hypotheses, distinctions, and requested next action. Intervening wording is omitted. Semantic annotations are proposed, not independently reviewed.

The graph deliberately does not claim that the listed dimensions form a canonical or MECE factorization. That is the open research question.

## Current frontier

Find the closest existing formal framework that can account for multiple forms of representation—including descriptive models, formal theories, mappings/interpretations, and executable or proof-producing systems—without assuming that one universally accepted canonical factorization already exists.

The fit review distinguishes this research question from Inquiry Graph's own schema. A single conversation can reveal a candidate representational gap but cannot authorize a shared schema change.
